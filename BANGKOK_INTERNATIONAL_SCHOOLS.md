# 🏫 Comprehensive Directory of International Schools in Bangkok, Thailand (2026 Guide)

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

Bangkok is one of Southeast Asia's leading hubs for international education, hosting over **120 international schools** catering to diverse expat and local Thai families. The schools range from centuries-old established institutions to ultra-modern campuses built in fast-growing suburban hubs.

### **Fee Structure Tiers (Annual Tuition)**
* **Premium Tier (THB 650,000 – 1,200,000+ / year):** Top-tier facilities, long heritage, high proportion of native expat faculty, extensive campus grounds (e.g. Wycombe Abbey, ISB, Patana, Shrewsbury, NIST, Harrow, Wellington, King's College).
* **Mid-Tier (THB 400,000 – 650,000 / year):** Excellent academic track record, modern facilities, balanced expat/local student ratio (e.g. Amnuay Silpa, Regent's, St. Andrews, KIS, RIS, Brighton College, Bangkok Prep).
* **Affordable / Budget Tier (THB 200,000 – 400,000 / year):** Focused academic programs, smaller campuses, highly cost-effective international education (e.g. Wells, SISB, Anglo Singapore, ICS).

*Note: Fees generally scale upwards with grade level. Secondary, IGCSE, A-Level, and IB Diploma years command the highest tuition. Additional one-off costs include Registration Fees (THB 50,000–250,000), Refundable/Non-refundable Capital Levies, Uniforms, Transport, and EAL (English as an Additional Language) support.*

## 💡 Practical Advice for Parents & Researchers

1. **Commute & Traffic Factors:**
   * Traffic congestion in Bangkok can turn a 5 km journey into a 60-minute ride during rush hours.
   * **BTS / Skytrain Accessible Schools:** Amnuay Silpa (Phaya Thai), Bangkok Prep (Thong Lo / On Nut), St. Andrews (Phra Khanong / Sukhumvit 71), NIST (Asok), Wells (On Nut / Thong Lo), Regent's Langsuan (Chidlom).
   * **Gated Expat Enclaves:** Nichada Thani (ISB) allows kids to safely bicycle or drive golf carts directly to school.

2. **Application Timelines:**
   * Academic years start in **August/September** (Term 1) and mid-year intakes occur in **January** (Term 2).
   * Top tier schools (Wycombe Abbey, ISB, NIST, Bangkok Patana, Shrewsbury) often require applications **6 to 12 months ahead**.

3. **Curriculum Alignment:**
   * **Planning for UK Universities:** British Curriculum (A-Levels) or IB Diploma are ideal.
   * **Planning for US Universities:** American Curriculum (AP) or IB Diploma are preferred.
   * **Thinking Skills & Metacognition:** Amnuay Silpa leads the region in structured cognitive thinking tools integrated with UK/Thai curricula.
