# MC5.3 Circles: Circumference & Area | Save My Exams Revision Notes

## 1. Core Intuition & Mental Model ("What is it?")

A circle is defined as the set of all points in a 2D plane that are equidistant from a central point.
* **Radius ($r$):** The distance from the center to any point on the boundary edge.
* **Diameter ($d$):** A line segment passing through the center connecting two opposite points on the edge ($d = 2r$).
* **Circumference ($C$):** The total perimeter (boundary distance) around the circle.
* **Area ($A$):** The total 2D surface space enclosed by the circle.

The mathematical constant $\pi$ (pi) represents the ratio of any circle's circumference to its diameter:
$$\pi = \frac{C}{d} \approx 3.14159265...$$

---

## 2. Key Definitions, Principles & Formulas

### Circumference of a Circle
$$C = \pi d = 2\pi r$$

### Area of a Circle
$$A = \pi r^2$$

### Semi-Circle Formulas
* **Perimeter of Semi-Circle:** Includes the curved arc length PLUS the straight diameter baseline.
  $$P_{\text{semi}} = \pi r + d = \pi r + 2r = r(\pi + 2)$$
* **Area of Semi-Circle:** Exactly half the area of a full circle.
  $$A_{\text{semi}} = \frac{1}{2}\pi r^2$$

### Quarter-Circle Formulas
* **Perimeter of Quarter-Circle:**
  $$P_{\text{quarter}} = \frac{1}{2}\pi r + 2r$$
* **Area of Quarter-Circle:**
  $$A_{\text{quarter}} = \frac{1}{4}\pi r^2$$

> 💡 **Exam Tip**: In Cambridge IGCSE Paper 1 and Paper 3 (Core), if $\pi$ is not specified as a fraction, use the standard calculator $\pi$ button or $3.142$. Round final non-exact answers to **$3$ significant figures**.

## 3. Step-by-Step Method & Worked Exam Demonstration

### Step-by-Step Method: Working Backwards from Area to Perimeter
1. **Identify Given Value:** Note whether area $A$ or circumference $C$ is provided.
2. **Rearrange Formula for Radius $r$:**
   * From Area: $r = \sqrt{\frac{A}{\pi}}$
   * From Circumference: $r = \frac{C}{2\pi}$
3. **Substitute & Calculate $r$:** Use full calculator precision.
4. **Evaluate Target Quantity:** Substitute $r$ into target circumference or area formula.

---

### Worked Exam Question

**(a)** A semi-circular garden pond has a radius of $r = 4.8\text{ m}$. Calculate:
  i) The total boundary perimeter of the pond.
  ii) The surface area of the pond.

**(b)** A circular brass plate has a total surface area of $154\text{ cm}^2$. Calculate the circumference of the plate.

#### Solution:

**(a) i) Perimeter of Semi-Circular Pond ($r = 4.8\text{ m}$):**
1. Curved arc length:
   $$\text{Arc} = \pi r = \pi \times 4.8 = 15.0796...\text{ m}$$
2. Add the straight diameter $d = 2r = 2 \times 4.8 = 9.6\text{ m}$:
   $$\text{Perimeter} = \pi r + 2r = 15.0796 + 9.6 = 24.6796...\text{ m}$$
3. Round to $3$ significant figures:
   $$\text{Perimeter} = 24.7\text{ m}$$

**(a) ii) Area of Semi-Circular Pond:**
1. Apply semi-circle area formula:
   $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\pi r^2 = \frac{1}{2} \times \pi \times (4.8)^2$$
   $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \pi \times 23.04 = 11.52\pi = 36.1911...\text{ m}^2$$
2. Round to $3$ significant figures:
   $$\text{Area} = 36.2\text{ m}^2$$

**(b) Circumference from Area ($A = 154\text{ cm}^2$):**
1. Set up area equation and solve for $r$:
   $$A = \pi r^2 = 154$$
   $$r^2 = \frac{154}{\pi} = 49.0197...$$
   $$r = \sqrt{49.0197...} = 7.0014...\text{ cm}$$

2. Substitute $r$ into circumference formula:
   $$C = 2\pi r = 2 \times \pi \times 7.0014... = 44.0088...\text{ cm}$$

3. Round to $3$ significant figures:
   $$C = 44.0\text{ cm}$$

## 4. Common Misconceptions & Diagnostic Exam Pitfalls

> ⚠️ **Common Misconception**: Forgetting the straight baseline $2r$ when finding the perimeter of a semi-circle.
* **Incorrect:** $\text{Perimeter} = \pi \times 4.8 = 15.1\text{ m}$ ❌ (Only calculates curved arc!)
* **Correct:** $\text{Perimeter} = \pi \times 4.8 + 2(4.8) = 24.7\text{ m}$ ✅

> ⚠️ **Common Misconception**: Squaring $2\pi r$ or failing to square $r$ in area calculations.
* **Incorrect:** $\text{Area} = \pi \times d^2$ or $(\pi r)^2$ ❌
* **Correct:** $\text{Area} = \pi \times (r)^2$ ✅

## 5. Key Terms & Revision Summary

| Component | Definition | Mathematical Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Radius ($r$)** | Distance from center to outer boundary. | $r = \frac{d}{2} = \sqrt{\frac{A}{\pi}}$ |
| **Diameter ($d$)** | Full distance across circle through center. | $d = 2r = \frac{C}{\pi}$ |
| **Circumference ($C$)** | Total perimeter of circle. | $C = 2\pi r = \pi d$ |
| **Full Circle Area ($A$)** | Enclosed 2D surface area. | $A = \pi r^2$ |
| **Semi-Circle Perimeter** | Curved half-circumference plus diameter. | $P = \pi r + 2r$ |
| **Semi-Circle Area** | Half of full circle area. | $A = \frac{1}{2}\pi r^2$ |
