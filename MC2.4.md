# Topic: MC2.4 Linear Equations & Inequalities

A linear equation statement equates two expressions to determine a single unknown value \(x\), whereas a linear inequality defines a range of values satisfying an ordering relation like \(ax + b \le c\).

---

### 1. Inequality Symbols & Equation Properties Breakdown

| Symbol / Concept | Meaning | Graphical Representation on Number Line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \(>\) / \(<\) | Strictly greater than / Strictly less than | Open circle (\(\circ\)) at boundary point |
| \(\ge\) / \(\le\) | Greater than or equal / Less than or equal | Solid filled circle (\(\bullet\)) at boundary point |
| Negative Reversal | Multiplying/dividing inequality by a negative flips sign. | \(-2x < 6 \implies x > -3\) |

---

### 2. Anatomy of Solving Linear Equations & Inequalities

```
                 Variable Term           Constant Term
                    [ 5x ]  -  7   =   [ 2x ]  +  8
                      |                   |
               Subtract 2x             Add 7
                      v                   v
                    [ 3x ]         =     [ 15 ]
                                   v
                             Divide by 3
                                   v
                             [ x ] = 5
```

* **Balancing Method:** Perform identical inverse operations to both sides of the equation to isolate variable terms.
* **Inequality Flip Rule:** Whenever multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number, reverse the direction of the inequality symbol.

$$-3x \le 12 \implies x \ge -4$$

---

### 3. Worked Example

> **Question:**
> 1. Solve the linear equation: \(5x - 7 = 2x + 8\).
> 2. Solve the linear inequality: \(3 - 2x < 11\) and represent the solution on a number line.

**Step 1: Solve linear equation with unknowns on both sides**

1. Subtract \(2x\) from both sides:

$$3x - 7 = 8$$

2. Add \(7\) to both sides and divide by 3:

$$3x = 15 \implies x = 5$$

**Step 2: Solve linear inequality with negative coefficient**

1. Subtract \(3\) from both sides:

$$-2x < 8$$

2. Divide both sides by \(-2\) and reverse the inequality sign:

$$x > \frac{8}{-2} \implies x > -4$$

3. **Key result 1:** The solution to the equation is \(x = 5\).
4. **Key result 2:** The solution to the inequality is \(x > -4\) (represented with an open circle at \(-4\) pointing right).

---

### 4. Common Examiner Traps

* **Forgetting Sign Reversal:** Dividing an inequality by a negative number without reversing the inequality direction, e.g., \(-2x < 8 \implies x < -4\) (incorrect).
* **Open vs Closed Circle Confusion:** Using a solid circle for strict inequalities (\(>\) or \(<\)) instead of an open circle on a number line.
