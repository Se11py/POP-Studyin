# Topic: MC2.9 Graphical Solutions of Equations

Graphical solution of equations involves identifying the \(x\)-coordinates of points where two graphed functions \(y = f(x)\) and \(y = g(x)\) intersect, satisfying \(f(x) = g(x)\).

---

### 1. Graphical Solution Methods Breakdown

| Solution Objective | Graphical Method | Result Read From Graph |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roots of \(f(x) = 0\) | Find points where curve \(y = f(x)\) crosses \(x\)-axis (\(y = 0\)). | \(x\)-intercept coordinates |
| Intersection of \(f(x) = g(x)\) | Draw line \(y = g(x)\) and find intersection points with curve \(y = f(x)\). | \(x\)-coordinates of intersection points |
| Simultaneous Equations | Plot both lines/curves; solution is matching point \((x, y)\). | Both \(x\) and \(y\) values of intersection |

---

### 2. Anatomy of Graphical Intersection

```
                        y
                        |       y = x + 2 (Line)
                   (2,4)*  /
                        | /
               ---------+--------- x
                       /|
                      / |   y = x² - 2 (Curve)
            Intersection at x = -1 and x = 2
```

* **Intersection Principle:** At the point of intersection, both graph equations share identical \((x, y)\) coordinate pairs.
* **Accuracy Requirement:** Solutions must be read to the grid accuracy requested (typically \(\pm 0.1\) grid units).

$$x^2 - 2 = x + 2 \implies x^2 - x - 4 = 0$$

---

### 3. Worked Example

> **Question:**
> The curve \(y = x^2 - 4x + 1\) is drawn on a grid.
> 1. Use the graph to estimate the solutions to \(x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0\).
> 2. By drawing a suitable straight line on the grid, solve \(x^2 - 4x + 1 = 2\).

**Step 1: Estimate roots of f(x) = 0 from x-intercepts**

1. Locate points where the curve intersects the line \(y = 0\) (\(x\)-axis):

$$\text{First intersection point: } x \approx 0.27$$

$$\text{Second intersection point: } x \approx 3.73$$

**Step 2: Solve f(x) = k by drawing line y = k**

1. Draw horizontal line \(y = 2\) across the coordinate grid.
2. Read the \(x\)-coordinates of the two intersection points between \(y = x^2 - 4x + 1\) and \(y = 2\):

$$x_1 \approx -0.2, \quad x_2 \approx 4.2$$

3. **Key result 1:** Roots of \(x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0\) are \(x \approx 0.3\) and \(x \approx 3.7\).
4. **Key result 2:** Solutions to \(x^2 - 4x + 1 = 2\) are \(x \approx -0.2\) and \(x \approx 4.2\).

---

### 4. Common Examiner Traps

* **Reading y-Value Instead of x-Value:** Giving the \(y\)-coordinate of the intersection point instead of the requested \(x\)-solution.
* **Inaccurate Line Placement:** Drawing freehand straight lines instead of using a ruler for linear equations like \(y = k\) or \(y = mx + c\).
