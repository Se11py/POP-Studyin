# POP Arena 🏟️ — Product & Gameplay Overview

**POP Arena** is a fast-paced, live multiplayer quiz game built directly into **POP Studying**. Inspired by popular games like Kahoot and Blooket, POP Arena brings high-energy competition into secondary school classrooms and study groups across Bangkok for IGCSE and A-Level revision.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Instant Room Joining**: Students join live games in seconds on their smartphones, tablets, or laptops by entering a simple 6-digit Game PIN.
* **Direct Study Integration**: Teachers and study group leaders can turn any POP Studying topic journey, flashcard set, or IGCSE/A-Level question bank into a live multiplayer game with a single click.
* **Live Classroom Projector View**: A dedicated host view designed for classroom projectors displaying live player joins, animated question timers, answer distributions, and leaderboard reveals.
* **Distraction-Free Game View**: A clean, fullscreen game interface focused entirely on fast choices, high-visibility visual symbols, and active study.

## 🎮 Gameplay Flow

1. **Host Launches a Game**: A teacher or host selects an IGCSE Physics or Math deck on POP Studying and clicks **Host POP Arena**.
2. **Students Join**: A 6-digit PIN appears on screen. Students open POP Arena on their phones, enter the PIN, choose a nickname, and pick a fun avatar.
3. **Question Round**: Questions appear on the main classroom screen. Students' phones transform into 4 vibrant, high-contrast touchpads (Red Triangle, Blue Diamond, Yellow Circle, Green Square).
4. **Instant Feedback & Streaks**: As time expires, the correct answer is revealed. Students see their score, points earned, and active answer streak (🔥 3 in a row!).
5. **Leaderboard & Podium**: After each question, the top 5 leaderboard updates live. At the end of the match, an animated 3D podium celebrates the top 3 champions!

---

## 🚀 Game Modes

* **Classic Arena (Kahoot Style)**: Fast-paced question rounds with live timers, streak bonuses, and classroom leaderboards.
* **Quest & Mini-Games (Blooket Style)**: Correct answers grant in-game coins that players use to unlock power-ups and play mini-games during review sessions.
