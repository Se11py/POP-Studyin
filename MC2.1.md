# Topic: MC2.1 Algebraic Representation & Formulae

Algebraic representation uses letters (variables) to express numbers and mathematical relationships in generalized form, where expressions like \(A = \pi r^2\) define a dependent subject \(A\) in terms of independent variables.

---

### 1. Algebraic Terminology Breakdown

| Term | Mathematical Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Variable / Symbol | A letter representing an unknown quantity or changing value. | \(x, y, t\) |
| Term | A single mathematical entity composed of numbers, variables, or their product. | \(3x^2, -5y, 7\) |
| Formula / Expression | Formula has an equal sign connecting variables (\(V = lwh\)); Expression has no equal sign (\(3x + 4\)). | \(P = 2l + 2w\) vs \(2x - 9\) |

---

### 2. Anatomy of a Formula & Substitution Structure

```
         Subject (Isolated on LHS)
            |
            v
          [ P ] = 2 [ l ] + 2 [ w ]
                    ^         ^
                    |         |
           Independent Variables (Substituted on RHS)
```

* **Subject of a Formula:** The single variable isolated on one side of the equal sign with a coefficient of positive 1.
* **Substitution Principle:** Replacing variable symbols with given numeric values inside parentheses to evaluate expressions safely.

$$P = 2(8.5) + 2(4.2) = 17 + 8.4 = 25.4$$

---

### 3. Worked Example

> **Question:**
> 1. The formula for the kinetic energy of an object is given by \(E = \frac{1}{2}mv^2\). Find the value of \(E\) when \(m = 6\text{ kg}\) and \(v = 5\text{ m/s}\).
> 2. Rearrange the formula \(P = 3x + 4y\) to make \(x\) the subject.

**Step 1: Evaluate energy using substitution**

1. Substitute \(m = 6\) and \(v = 5\) into the energy equation:

$$E = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times (5)^2$$

2. Calculate the squared velocity and evaluate the final product:

$$E = 3 \times 25 = 75$$

**Step 2: Change the subject of the formula to \(x\)**

1. Subtract \(4y\) from both sides to isolate the term containing \(x\):

$$P - 4y = 3x$$

2. Divide both sides by 3 to isolate \(x\):

$$x = \frac{P - 4y}{3}$$

3. **Key result 1:** The kinetic energy \(E = 75\text{ J}\).
4. **Key result 2:** The rearranged formula is \(x = \frac{P - 4y}{3}\).

---

### 4. Common Examiner Traps

* **Negative Substitution Error:** Forgetting to square negative values inside parentheses, e.g., evaluating \((-3)^2\) as \(-9\) instead of \(+9\).
* **Incomplete Division:** Dividing only one term instead of the entire opposite expression when isolating a variable, e.g., writing \(x = P - \frac{4y}{3}\) instead of \(x = \frac{P - 4y}{3}\).
