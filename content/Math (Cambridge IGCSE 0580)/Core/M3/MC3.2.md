# MC3.2 Drawing Straight-Line Graphs | Save My Exams Revision Notes

## 1. Core Intuition & Mental Model ("What is it?")

A straight-line graph is a visual representation of a linear relationship between two variables, $x$ and $y$. Whenever $x$ changes by a constant amount, $y$ also changes by a constant amount. 

To draw a straight-line graph accurately, you need to calculate coordinates for at least two points (plus a third point as a check) using a table of values, plot these points on a grid, and join them using a ruler to create a continuous straight line that extends across the given domain.

---

## 2. Key Definitions, Principles & Formulas

### General Equation of a Straight Line
$$y = mx + c$$
- $m$: Gradient (steepness of the line)
- $c$: $y$-intercept (the point $(0, c)$ where the line crosses the $y$-axis)

### Horizontal and Vertical Special Lines
- **Horizontal Lines**: $y = k$ (where $k$ is a constant). The line passes horizontally through $(0, k)$ with gradient $m = 0$.
- **Vertical Lines**: $x = k$ (where $k$ is a constant). The line passes vertically through $(k, 0)$ with undefined gradient.

### Table of Values Structure
Choose values of $x$ (often given in the exam domain like $-3 \le x \le 3$), substitute each $x$-value into the line equation to calculate the corresponding $y$-value, and form coordinate pairs $(x, y)$.

> 💡 **Exam Tip**: Always calculate at least $3$ points. If all $3$ points line up in a straight line, your calculations are correct! If one point is off the line, re-check your arithmetic.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Method & Worked Exam Demonstration

### Step-by-Step Method for Drawing Straight-Line Graphs
1. **Construct a Table of Values**: Select $3$ or $4$ values for $x$ across the requested domain.
2. **Substitute $x$ to find $y$**: Substitute each chosen $x$-value into the equation $y = mx + c$.
3. **Write as Coordinate Pairs**: Group corresponding $(x, y)$ values.
4. **Plot Points**: Mark each point on the Cartesian coordinate grid using a sharp pencil and neat cross $\times$.
5. **Draw the Line**: Use a ruler to draw a single continuous straight line through all plotted points. Extend the line slightly past the outermost points to cover the entire grid domain.

---

### Worked Exam Question

**Question**:
(a) Complete the table of values for the straight line $y = 3x - 2$ for $-2 \le x \le 3$.

| $x$ | $-2$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $y$ | | $-5$ | | $1$ | | $7$ |

(b) On a Cartesian grid, draw the graph of $y = 3x - 2$ for $-2 \le x \le 3$.

#### Solution & Step-by-Step Explanation:

**(a) Completing the Table of Values**:
- **Step 1**: For $x = -2$:
  $$y = 3(-2) - 2 = -6 - 2 = -8$$
- **Step 2**: For $x = 0$:
  $$y = 3(0) - 2 = 0 - 2 = -2$$
- **Step 3**: For $x = 2$:
  $$y = 3(2) - 2 = 6 - 2 = 4$$

Completed table:

| $x$ | $-2$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $y$ | $-8$ | $-5$ | $-2$ | $1$ | $4$ | $7$ |

**(b) Drawing the Graph**:
- **Step 1**: Identify coordinates to plot: $(-2, -8)$, $(-1, -5)$, $(0, -2)$, $(1, 1)$, $(2, 4)$, and $(3, 7)$.
- **Step 2**: Plot each point accurately on the coordinate grid with a small cross $\times$.
- **Step 3**: Place a ruler aligned across all points and draw a clean, continuous straight line from $x = -2$ to $x = 3$.
- **Step 4**: Label the drawn line as $y = 3x - 2$.

## 4. Common Misconceptions & Diagnostic Exam Pitfalls

> ⚠️ **Common Misconception**: Stopping the line strictly at the plotted points rather than drawing a continuous line across the entire grid range. Examiners require the line to cover the full specified domain $-2 \le x \le 3$.

> ⚠️ **Common Misconception**: Arithmetic errors when substituting negative $x$-values into negative coefficients, such as calculating $y = -2(-3)$ as $-6$ instead of $+6$. Always use brackets: $-2 \times (-3) = +6$.

> 💡 **Exam Tip**: If an equation is written in implicit form like $2x + y = 6$, rearrange it to $y = -2x + 6$ before filling out your table of values.

## 5. Key Terms & Revision Summary

| Term | Definition | Formula / Representation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Linear Graph** | A graph that forms a single non-curved straight line | $y = mx + c$ |
| **Table of Values** | A grid used to calculate $y$-outputs from chosen $x$-inputs | $x$-row and $y$-row |
| **$y$-intercept** | The point where the line crosses the vertical axis | $(0, c)$ |
| **Domain** | The range of $x$-values specified for drawing the graph | e.g. $-3 \le x \le 3$ |
