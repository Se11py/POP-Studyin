# C1.16 Currency Conversion & Financial Mathematics (Core Tier)

## Financial Calculations in Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics

Financial mathematics tests your ability to handle real-world money scenarios—such as exchanging currencies, calculating simple interest on investments or loans, and determining net profit or loss.

---

## 1. Currency Conversion & Exchange Rates

An **exchange rate** states how much one unit of currency is worth in another currency.

$$\text{Currency A} \times \text{Exchange Rate} = \text{Currency B}$$

$$\text{Currency B} \div \text{Exchange Rate} = \text{Currency A}$$

*To avoid confusion*: Use a simple proportions setup $\frac{\text{Currency A}}{\text{Currency B}} = \frac{1}{\text{Rate}}$.

---

## 2. Simple Interest Formula

**Simple interest** calculates interest earned or paid strictly on the **original principal amount ($P$)** every year. The interest added each year remains constant.

$$I = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

* **$I$**: Simple Interest earned/paid ($$)
* **$P$**: Principal amount (original sum invested or borrowed) ($$)
* **$R$**: Annual Interest Rate ($\%$)
* **$T$**: Time period ($\text{years}$)

$$\text{Total Future Value (A)} = \text{Principal } P + \text{Interest } I$$

## Step-by-Step Worked Exam Demonstrations

### Question 1: Currency Exchange Calculation
The exchange rate between US Dollars ($\$$) and Euros ($€$) is $\$1 = 0.92\text{ Euros}$.

(a) Convert $\$450$ into Euros.  
(b) Convert $€644$ into US Dollars.

#### Solution & Line-by-Line Annotations

**(a) Converting $\$450$ to Euros:**
1. Multiply by exchange rate:
   $$\text{Euros} = 450 \times 0.92 = 414\text{ Euros}$$

**(b) Converting $€644$ to US Dollars:**
1. Divide by exchange rate:
   $$\text{Dollars} = \frac{644}{0.92} = \$700$$

---

### Question 2: Simple Interest Problem
Sarah invests $\$1500$ in a bank account paying simple interest at a rate of $4.5\%$ per year. Calculate the total amount in her account after $3$ years.

#### Solution & Line-by-Line Annotations

1. **Step 1: Calculate Simple Interest ($I = \frac{PRT}{100}$)**:
   * $P = 1500$
   * $R = 4.5$
   * $T = 3$
   $$I = \frac{1500 \times 4.5 \times 3}{100} = 15 \times 4.5 \times 3 = \$202.50$$

2. **Step 2: Calculate Total Amount ($A = P + I$)**:
   $$A = \$1500 + \$202.50 = \$1702.50$$

## Examiner Traps & Common Candidate Pitfalls

* **Mistake: Writing Currency Amounts to 1 Decimal Place**  
  *Why it happens*: Calculator displays `$202.5`, so the candidate writes `$202.5` on the answer line.  
  *The Correction*: Money MUST **always** be written to 2 decimal places (e.g. `$202.50`). Leaving a trailing zero off money values loses the final accuracy mark!

* **Mistake: Forgetting to Add Principal to Find Total Amount**  
  *Why it happens*: Calculating interest $I = \$202.50$ and stopping there when the prompt asks for "total amount in the account".  
  *The Correction*: Interest is only the profit! Total amount = Principal + Interest ($1500 + 202.50 = \$1702.50$).
