# C1.11 Ratio & Sharing in a Given Ratio (Core Tier)

## What Is a Ratio and How Does Sharing Work?

A **ratio** compares the relative sizes of two or more quantities of the same unit (e.g. $2 : 3 : 5$). Ratios show how many times one quantity contains another, without specifying the actual physical amounts.

Sharing a total quantity in a given ratio means dividing the total into equal **parts** or "shares", and then distributing those parts according to the ratio numbers.

---

## The 3-Step Ratio Sharing Method

To divide a total amount $T$ in the ratio $a : b : c$:

1. **Step 1: Add the Parts**  
   Find the total number of equal shares:
   $$\text{Total Parts} = a + b + c$$

2. **Step 2: Find the Value of 1 Part**  
   Divide the total amount by the total number of parts:
   $$\text{Value of 1 Part} = \frac{\text{Total Amount } T}{\text{Total Parts}}$$

3. **Step 3: Multiply to Find Each Share**  
   Multiply the value of 1 part by each ratio number:
   $$\text{Share } A = a \times \text{Value of 1 Part}$$
   $$\text{Share } B = b \times \text{Value of 1 Part}$$
   $$\text{Share } C = c \times \text{Value of 1 Part}$$

*Sanity Check*: The sum of all individual shares must equal the original total amount $T$!

## Step-by-Step Worked Exam Demonstrations

### Question 1: Sharing a Total Amount
Share $\$450$ between Alice, Ben, and Charlie in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$.

#### Solution & Line-by-Line Annotations

1. **Step 1: Find total parts**:
   $$\text{Total Parts} = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9\text{ parts}$$

2. **Step 2: Find value of 1 part**:
   $$\text{Value of 1 Part} = \frac{\$450}{9} = \$50$$

3. **Step 3: Calculate individual shares**:
   * **Alice ($2$ parts)**: $2 \times \$50 = \$100$
   * **Ben ($3$ parts)**: $3 \times \$50 = \$150$
   * **Charlie ($4$ parts)**: $4 \times \$50 = \$200$

4. **Verify total**: $\$100 + \$150 + \$200 = \$450 \quad (\text{Verified!})$

---

### Question 2: Given One Person's Share (The Difference Problem)
Alice and Ben share money in the ratio $5 : 8$. Given that Ben receives $\$36$ more than Alice, calculate how much money Alice receives.

#### Solution & Line-by-Line Annotations

1. **Find the difference in parts**:
   $$\text{Difference in Parts} = 8 - 5 = 3\text{ parts}$$

2. **Equate part difference to the dollar difference**:
   $$3\text{ parts} = \$36 \implies 1\text{ part} = \frac{\$36}{3} = \$12$$

3. **Calculate Alice's share ($5$ parts)**:
   $$\text{Alice's Share} = 5 \times \$12 = \$60$$

## Examiner Traps & Common Candidate Pitfalls

* **Mistake: Dividing the Total by the First Ratio Number**  
  *Why it happens*: In $2 : 3 : 4$, candidates divide $\$450$ by $2$.  
  *The Correction*: You MUST divide the total by the **sum of all ratio parts** ($2+3+4 = 9$), not just one number.

* **Mistake: Misreading "Given One Share" vs "Given Total"**  
  *Why it happens*: Treating $\$36$ as the total sum in Question 2 above.  
  *The Correction*: Read carefully! If the prompt says "Ben gets $\$36$ *more*", $\$36$ corresponds to the **difference in parts** ($8 - 5 = 3$), NOT the total.
