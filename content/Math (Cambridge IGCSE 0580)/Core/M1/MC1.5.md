# C1.5 Ordering & Inequality Symbols (Core Tier)

## Comparing Numbers Across Different Formats

In Cambridge IGCSE examination questions, ordering numbers requires ranking a mixed collection of fractions, negative numbers, decimals, percentages, and roots from smallest to largest (ascending) or largest to smallest (descending).

To compare quantities accurately, you must convert all numbers into a single common language—typically **decimal notation** to 3 or 4 decimal places—and use inequality symbols to state their relationship.

---

## Inequality Symbols Reference Table

| Symbol | Name / Meaning | Example Statement | True Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `=` | Equals | $x = 5$ | $x$ is exactly equal to $5$ |
| $\neq$ | Not equal to | $x \neq 3$ | $x$ can be any value except $3$ |
| $<$ | Strictly less than | $x < 8$ | $x$ is strictly smaller than $8$ (does not include $8$) |
| $>$ | Strictly greater than | $x > 2$ | $x$ is strictly larger than $2$ (does not include $2$) |
| $\le$ | Less than or equal to | $x \le 10$ | $x$ is at most $10$ (includes $10$) |
| $\ge$ | Greater than or equal to | $x \ge 4$ | $x$ is at least $4$ (includes $4$) |

*Memory Trick for Symbols*: The inequality symbol always points its small arrow tip at the **smaller number** ($3 < 7$).

## Step-by-Step Worked Exam Demonstrations

### Question 1: Ordering Real Numbers
Insert the correct symbol ($<$, $>$, or $=$) between each pair of values:
(a) $-8 \quad \square \quad -5$  
(b) $\frac{5}{8} \quad \square \quad 0.625$  
(c) $\sqrt{0.49} \quad \square \quad 70\%$

#### Solution & Line-by-Line Annotations

**(a) Comparing $-8$ and $-5$:**
On a horizontal number line, numbers further to the left are smaller. $-8$ is further left than $-5$:
$$-8 < -5$$

**(b) Comparing $\frac{5}{8}$ and $0.625$:**
1. Convert fraction to decimal: $5 \div 8 = 0.625$.
2. Both quantities are identical:
   $$\frac{5}{8} = 0.625$$

**(c) Comparing $\sqrt{0.49}$ and $70\%$:**
1. Evaluate root: $\sqrt{0.49} = 0.7$.
2. Convert percentage to decimal: $70\% = 70 \div 100 = 0.7$.
3. Both quantities are identical:
   $$\sqrt{0.49} = 70\%$$

---

### Question 2: Ascending Order Problem
Write the following numbers in order of size, starting with the smallest:
$$-\frac{3}{4}, \quad 0.72, \quad -0.8, \quad \sqrt{0.5}, \quad 71\%$$

#### Solution & Line-by-Line Annotations

1. Convert every value into decimal form:
   * $-\frac{3}{4} = -0.7500$
   * $0.72 = 0.7200$
   * $-0.8 = -0.8000$
   * $\sqrt{0.5} \approx 0.7071$
   * $71\% = 0.7100$

2. Order decimals from smallest (most negative) to largest (most positive):
   $$-0.8000 < -0.7500 < 0.7071 < 0.7100 < 0.7200$$

3. Substitute back the original prompt representations:
   $$-0.8, \quad -\frac{3}{4}, \quad \sqrt{0.5}, \quad 71\%, \quad 0.72$$

## Examiner Traps & Common Candidate Pitfalls

* **Mistake: Negative Number Ordering Errors**  
  *Why it happens*: Candidates see $8 > 5$ and assume $-8 > -5$.  
  *The Correction*: Negative numbers work in reverse! $-8$ represents a lower value (colder temperature / larger debt) than $-5$, so $-8 < -5$.

* **Mistake: Ascending vs Descending Direction**  
  *Why it happens*: Misreading the prompt instruction.  
  *The Correction*: **Ascending** means climbing up (smallest to largest). **Descending** means going down (largest to smallest).
