# E1.13 Reverse Percentages & Multiplier Algebra (Extended Tier)

## What Is a Reverse Percentage Problem?

In Extended Tier Mathematics, a **reverse percentage** problem asks you to find the **original initial value ($100\%$)** *before* a percentage increase or decrease took place.

In standard percentage problems, you know the original value and multiply by the multiplier to find the new value. In reverse percentage problems, you know the **final sale price, taxed amount, or inflated population** and must work backwards using division:

$$\text{Original Value} \times \text{Multiplier} = \text{Final Value} \implies \text{Original Value} = \frac{\text{Final Value}}{\text{Multiplier}}$$

---

## Reverse Percentage Multipliers Reference Table

| Scenario | Given Final Condition | Percentage Representation | Multiplier Equation | Original Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$20\%$ Sale Reduction** | Final price is $\$160$ | $100\% - 20\% = 80\%$ | $\text{Orig} \times 0.80 = 160$ | $\text{Orig} = \frac{160}{0.80} = \$200$ |
| **$15\%$ Price Increase** | Final price is $\$230$ | $100\% + 15\% = 115\%$ | $\text{Orig} \times 1.15 = 230$ | $\text{Orig} = \frac{230}{1.15} = \$200$ |
| **$8\%$ Tax Addition (VAT)** | Total paid is $\$540$ | $100\% + 8\% = 108\%$ | $\text{Orig} \times 1.08 = 540$ | $\text{Orig} = \frac{540}{1.08} = \$500$ |
| **$12\%$ Population Loss** | Final count is $4400$ | $100\% - 12\% = 88\%$ | $\text{Orig} \times 0.88 = 4400$ | $\text{Orig} = \frac{4400}{0.88} = 5000$ |

## Step-by-Step Worked Exam Demonstrations

### Question 1: Reverse Percentage Sale Reduction
In a clearance sale, all items are reduced by $35\%$. The sale price of a laptop is $\$585$. Calculate the original price of the laptop before the sale.

#### Solution & Line-by-Line Annotations

1. **Step 1: Determine the percentage remaining**:
   $$\text{Sale Price Percentage} = 100\% - 35\% = 65\%$$

2. **Step 2: Express $65\%$ as a decimal multiplier**:
   $$\text{Multiplier} = 0.65$$

3. **Step 3: Set up equation**:
   $$\text{Original Price} \times 0.65 = \$585$$

4. **Step 4: Divide final price by multiplier**:
   $$\text{Original Price} = \frac{\$585}{0.65} = \$900$$

---

### Question 2: Reverse Percentage Tax Addition
A camera costs $\$486$ including an $8\%$ Sales Tax. Calculate the pre-tax cost of the camera.

#### Solution & Line-by-Line Annotations

1. **Determine total percentage including tax**:
   $$\text{Total Percentage} = 100\% + 8\% = 108\% = 1.08$$

2. **Set up algebraic equation**:
   $$\text{Pre-Tax Price} \times 1.08 = \$486$$

3. **Divide by multiplier**:
   $$\text{Pre-Tax Price} = \frac{\$486}{1.08} = \$450$$

## Examiner Traps & Common Candidate Pitfalls

* **Mistake: Applying the Percentage to the Final Value**  
  *Why it happens*: In Question 1, finding $35\%$ of $\$585$ ($\$204.75$) and adding it to $\$585$ ($\$789.75$).  
  *The Correction*: $35\%$ was taken off the **original price**, NOT the reduced sale price! You CANNOT take $35\%$ of $\$585$. You MUST divide by $0.65$.

* **Mistake: Subtracting Tax Percentage from Final Price**  
  *Why it happens*: Finding $8\%$ of $\$486$ ($\$38.88$) and subtracting it.  
  *The Correction*: Always use division: $\text{Original} = \frac{\text{Final}}{1 + \text{rate}}$.
