# E1.12 Advanced Rates, Unit Conversions & Density (Extended Tier)

## Extended Rates & Compound Unit Transformations

In Extended Tier Mathematics, rate problems involve multi-variable unit conversions (e.g. converting $\text{g/cm}^3$ to $\text{kg/m}^3$ or $\text{m/s}$ to $\text{km/h}$), density-mass-volume relationships, and multi-stage speed calculations.

---

## Key Rates & Density Formulas

| Concept / Rate | Standard Units | Governing Formula | Key Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Speed ($S$)** | $\text{m/s}$ or $\text{km/h}$ | $S = \frac{D}{T}$ | $\text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}}$ |
| **Density ($\rho$ or $d$)** | $\text{g/cm}^3$ or $\text{kg/m}^3$ | $\text{Density} = \frac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Volume}}$ | $M = d \cdot V, \quad V = \frac{M}{d}$ |
| **Pressure ($P$)** | $\text{N/m}^2$ or $\text{N/cm}^2$ | $\text{Pressure} = \frac{\text{Force}}{\text{Area}}$ | $F = P \cdot A, \quad A = \frac{F}{P}$ |

---

## Converting Compound Units ($\text{g/cm}^3 \leftrightarrow \text{kg/m}^3$)

1. **Converting $\text{g/cm}^3$ to $\text{kg/m}^3$**:
   * $1\text{ kg} = 1000\text{ g} \implies 1\text{ g} = 10^{-3}\text{ kg}$
   * $1\text{ m} = 100\text{ cm} \implies 1\text{ m}^3 = (100)^3\text{ cm}^3 = 10^6\text{ cm}^3 \implies 1\text{ cm}^3 = 10^{-6}\text{ m}^3$
   $$\text{Density in kg/m}^3 = \text{Density in g/cm}^3 \times 1000$$

## Step-by-Step Worked Exam Demonstrations

### Question 1: Density, Mass & Volume Problem
A solid metal cylinder has radius $r = 4\text{ cm}$ and height $h = 10\text{ cm}$. The density of the metal is $7.8\text{ g/cm}^3$.

(a) Calculate the volume of the cylinder in $\text{cm}^3$, giving your answer in terms of $\pi$.  
(b) Calculate the mass of the cylinder in kilograms.

#### Solution & Line-by-Line Annotations

**(a) Finding Volume of Cylinder ($V = \pi r^2 h$):**
$$V = \pi \times (4)^2 \times 10 = \pi \times 16 \times 10 = 160\pi\text{ cm}^3$$

**(b) Finding Mass in Kilograms ($\text{Mass} = \text{Density} \times \text{Volume}$):**

1. Calculate mass in grams ($g$):
   $$\text{Mass} = 7.8\text{ g/cm}^3 \times 160\pi\text{ cm}^3 = 1248\pi\text{ g} \approx 3920.707...\text{ g}$$

2. Convert grams to kilograms ($\div 1000$):
   $$\text{Mass in kg} = \frac{3920.707...}{1000} = 3.9207... \approx 3.92\text{ kg} \quad (3\text{ s.f.})$$

## Examiner Traps & Common Candidate Pitfalls

* **Mistake: Incorrect Conversion Factor for Cubic Units**  
  *Why it happens*: Dividing by $100$ instead of $100^3 = 1\,000\,000$ when converting $\text{cm}^3$ to $\text{m}^3$.  
  *The Correction*: Volume scaling uses **cubic factors**: $1\text{ m}^3 = 1\,000\,000\text{ cm}^3$.

* **Mistake: Forgetting to Convert Grams to Kilograms**  
  *Why it happens*: Leaving the final answer as $3920\text{ kg}$ when calculated in grams.  
  *The Correction*: Check question units! If mass is requested in **kilograms**, divide grams by $1000$.
