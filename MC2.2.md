# Topic: MC2.2 Algebraic Manipulation

Algebraic manipulation involves expanding brackets using the distributive property \(a(b + c) = ab + ac\) and simplifying expressions by collecting like terms that share identical variable components.

---

### 1. Algebraic Operations & Terms Breakdown

| Operation | Mathematical Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collecting Like Terms | Add or subtract coefficients of identical variable terms. | \(4x + 7x = 11x\); \(5a + 3b - 2a = 3a + 3b\) |
| Single Bracket Expansion | Multiply the term outside by every term inside: \(a(b + c) = ab + ac\). | \(3x(2x - 5) = 6x^2 - 15x\) |
| Single Factorisation | Extract the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of coefficients and variables. | \(12x^2 + 18x = 6x(2x + 3)\) |

---

### 2. Anatomy of Expanding & Factorising

```
                     EXPAND (Multiply across)
                 ------------------------------->
                 3x ( 2x  -  5 )  =  6x²  -  15x
                 <-------------------------------
                    FACTORISE (Take out HCF)
```

* **Distributive Expansion:** The exterior multiplier \(3x\) must operate on both \(2x\) and \(-5\) individually.
* **Highest Common Factor (HCF):** For \(6x^2 - 15x\), numerical HCF is \(3\) and variable HCF is \(x\), making total HCF \(6x / 2 = 3x\).

$$3x(2x - 5) \equiv 6x^2 - 15x$$

---

### 3. Worked Example

> **Question:**
> 1. Expand and simplify the expression: \(4(2x - 3) - 2(x - 5)\).
> 2. Factorise completely: \(15p^2q - 10pq^2\).

**Step 1: Expand and simplify linear brackets**

1. Expand the first bracket \(4(2x - 3)\) and second bracket \(-2(x - 5)\):

$$4(2x - 3) - 2(x - 5) = 8x - 12 - 2x + 10$$

2. Group and collect like terms:

$$(8x - 2x) + (-12 + 10) = 6x - 2$$

**Step 2: Factorise completely by extracting common factors**

1. Identify HCF of coefficients (\(15, 10 \rightarrow 5\)) and variables (\(p^2q, pq^2 \rightarrow pq\)):

$$\text{HCF} = 5pq$$

2. Divide each term by \(5pq\) to get inside expression:

$$15p^2q - 10pq^2 = 5pq(3p - 2q)$$

3. **Key result 1:** Simplified expression is \(6x - 2\).
4. **Key result 2:** Fully factorised form is \(5pq(3p - 2q)\).

---

### 4. Common Examiner Traps

* **Negative Bracket Expansion Error:** Failing to multiply a negative sign through all terms inside a bracket, e.g., \(-2(x - 5) = -2x - 10\) instead of \(-2x + 10\).
* **Incomplete Factorisation:** Leaving a common factor inside the bracket, such as writing \(5(3p^2q - 2pq^2)\) instead of fully extracting \(5pq(3p - 2q)\).
